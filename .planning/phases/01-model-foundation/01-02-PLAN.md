src/core/distribution.ts MUST export:
- `interface Curve { lognormal:{mu:number;sigma:number}; pareto:{alpha:number;xm:number}; stitchQuantile:number; totalWealth:number }`
- `calibrateCurve(anchors:Anchors):Curve` — solves (μ,σ) from body anchors, (α,xm) from upper anchors, continuity at stitch closes the system. Document which anchor pins which parameter (RESEARCH Pattern 4 / Assumption A3): median→μ; median+top10→σ; top1+top0.1→α; continuity→xm.
- `quantile(curve:Curve, p:number):number` — wealth at percentile p∈(0,1).
- `cdf(curve:Curve, w:number):number` — percentile of wealth w.
- `percentileOf(curve:Curve, w:number):number` — alias of cdf for engine readability (user moving-tier read, MODEL-02/D-06).
- `cumulativeShareFromTop(curve:Curve, p:number):number` — closed-form fraction of total wealth held by the top (1−p) mass (Pareto top-share + lognormal partial-expectation identity, RESEARCH Pattern 3). Strictly monotone in p.
- `dynamicTopSetPercentile(curve:Curve):number` — monotone bisection inverting `cumulativeShareFromTop = 0.50` to the exact interpolated percentile (D-03; NO discrete tier stepping).
- `returnAtPercentile(percentile:number, returnByTier:ReturnByTier):number` — monotone (e.g. monotone-PCHIP/log-linear) interpolation across the 4 anchor percentiles; strictly increasing in percentile (MODEL-02).
- A unit-tested erf/Φ helper (Abramowitz-Stegun 7.1.26) with its own table-of-known-values test; achievable precision documented against `DIST_TOL=1e-6` (RESEARCH Open Question 2 / Assumption A1), explicitly NOT the <1e-9 annuity tolerance.

Tolerances: distribution-math assertions use `DIST_TOL` from testUtils (1e-6), NOT relErr<1e-9 (that is reserved for the drag-off annuity golden master in Plan 03).
