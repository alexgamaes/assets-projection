<objective>
Build the selector + presentation layer for VIZ-07: the two memoized ECharts
option builders (`selectShareOption` 100%-stacked-area + user line,
`selectDonutOption` final-year donut), the `SHARE_CAPTION` constant, and the
two thin ReactECharts wrapper components — all driven by the `BandShare[]`

ranges, not median/top-10% point vocabulary. D-05: no user slice (user is
contained within their population band; a literal slice would double-count).

userPercentile mapped onto the cumulative-share axis.

pattern); the option construction is data-derived and belongs in the pure
selector module. Components are thin wrappers mirroring `RelativePosChart`.

</objective>
